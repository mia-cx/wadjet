# @wadjet/macos

## 0.0.1-alpha.2

### Patch Changes

- c24e86f: Settings has two capture defaults, both on: Freeze the screen while selecting, and Capture the cursor. With Freeze off, Screenshots are taken from the live desktop when you confirm.
- c24e86f: The toolbar's and menus' glass highlights follow the pointer.
- 02204ef: The pressed lens works like iOS 26's tab bar: the toolbar looks squeezed inside it, and Tools grow as they pass into it.
- c24e86f: Tool menu highlights sit inside the menu's rounded corners, and menu options are grouped by what they do.
- c24e86f: Take Screenshots while a Recording runs: the Recording gets its own menu bar item, and Tool menus look like iOS 26 context menus.
- c24e86f: Screenshot menus have Cursor and Wadjet tiles, so a capture can include or leave out the pointer and Wadjet's own toolbar.
- c24e86f: The Workflows settings pane lays out like the other tabs, the Freeze Frame appears without the system's zoom-in, and the toast thumbnail keeps the capture's shape.
- c24e86f: While Settings is open, Wadjet shows in the Dock, ⌘-Tab and Mission Control, and Capture Window can capture the Settings window.
- c24e86f: Shortcuts for Capture Window, Capture Screen, Record Window and Record Screen can skip the confirm and capture the focused window or the pointer's display straight away.
- c24e86f: Settings › Shortcuts lists every Tool, with its Shortcuts under it.
- c24e86f: Drag a toast's thumbnail into any app to drop the capture there like a file.
- a509c8e: The toolbar's glass edges are anti-aliased, the edge highlight is softer, and the group separators no longer touch the selected Tool's highlight.
